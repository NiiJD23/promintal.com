Home / About

Small by design. Senior by default.

Promintal Consulting Limited is a UK consultancy and software studio, registered in London. We lead the programmes that matter and build the tools that make them easier.

Who we are

Promintal exists for the moments when delivery cannot be allowed to fail: a flagship programme, a market launch, a transformation the board has staked its reputation on.

We are deliberately boutique. The person who scopes your engagement is the person who leads it, backed by a trusted network of specialists brought in only where they add value. Alongside consulting, we design and run our own software, so our advice is shaped by building and operating real products.

Promintal is an advisory and software company. We do not provide, invest in or underwrite funding for projects.

Our name carries our intent: professional integrity, applied with ascent in mind. The rising arrow in our logo is the direction we hold every programme to.
